The programme

Portugal's Golden Visa, explained.

The Residence Permit for Investment Activity (ARI) enables eligible non-EU/EEA/Swiss nationals to apply for Portuguese residence through qualifying investments, including certain non-real-estate investment funds.

The investment and the immigration application are distinct processes; fund eligibility does not automatically establish an applicant's eligibility for residence.

Questions investors ask

Before you get in touch.

Do I have to move to Portugal?

No. The minimum-stay requirement is commonly described as averaging seven days per year, so you can keep living and working abroad, subject to the applicable residency and citizenship requirements.

Can my family be included?

The programme provides for the possibility of including eligible family members.

Is my capital locked in?

The funds are open-ended with daily dealing and no lock-up period. To keep Golden Visa status, the statutory minimum investment-holding period is five years, subject to applicable rules. Daily dealing does not guarantee immediate payment of redemption proceeds.

Does investing guarantee the residence permit?

No. The investment and the immigration application are distinct processes. Fund eligibility does not automatically establish an applicant's eligibility for residence.

Does 3 Comma Capital handle the immigration application?

No. 3 Comma Capital is an investment manager, not an immigration or tax adviser. The team coordinates with your independent legal advisers where appropriate.

What are the fees and how long does it take?

Fund fees are set out in the official fund documents, which the investment team shares on request.
